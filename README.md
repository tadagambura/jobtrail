# JobTrail

**Track every job application without a spreadsheet**

> #31 of 33 · Wave 3: Seasonal and episodic · Used: Until hired · Easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Money & Work

## What it is

Log job applications and their stage (applied, interview, offer, rejected), set follow-up reminders, keep interview notes and hit a weekly applications goal. Your whole pipeline at a glance.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Job hunting is stressful and intense, and most people use messy spreadsheets. A weekly goal and pipeline view give a reason to open the app several times a week during a search. Strong with students and graduates in application season.

## Who it's for

Graduates, career changers and anyone applying for lots of roles.

**The problem:** Applications get lost, follow-ups are forgotten, and it's hard to see progress.

## Version one

- Applications with company, role, link, date and stage
- Pipeline board
- Follow-up and interview reminders
- Notes per application (questions, contacts)
- Weekly applications goal with a streak

**Example:** A graduate applies to 12 roles a week, gets nudged to follow up after 7 days, and sees three move to interview.

## Later

- Export to CSV

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Calm.** Gentle, kind humour and smooth motion: a stickman yawning after a shift, a fly escaping an empty pocket. No drama, nothing that adds stress.

- Application sent: a paper plane flies off
- Interview booked: the stickman straightens his tie in the mirror
- Offer: confetti and a huge jump (the only celebration; rejections are never animated)
- Follow-up due: an envelope taps its foot
- Weekly goal: the stickman climbs another rung of a ladder

**Line we don't cross:** Rejections are never animated.
